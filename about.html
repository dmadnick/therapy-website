I am a licensed psychologist in California and Pennsylvania, offering online therapy for teens, adults, and older adults. I specialize in evidence-based treatment for anxiety, OCD, trauma, phobias, social anxiety, health anxiety, and depression. I have a particular focus on perinatal anxiety and OCD in women, and on helping people of all ages cope with chronic medical conditions.

I received my Psy.D. in Clinical Psychology from La Salle University, with a focus on health psychology. I completed my APA-accredited predoctoral internship at Kaiser Permanente Los Angeles Medical Center, and I have also practiced as a licensed psychologist at the CBT Center for Anxiety and OCD.

My approach to therapy is warm and direct. I bring compassion, humor, and flexibility to our work, because I believe a strong therapeutic relationship is the foundation for lasting change. I draw from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), tailoring treatment to your needs and goals.

Outside of my work, I enjoy hiking with my dog, spending time with family and friends, and reading.
